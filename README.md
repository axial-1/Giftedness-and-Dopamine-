## A Brief Analysis of Dopamine and Giftedness 

To begin with, the first thing to explore is exactly how dopamine functions—whether it’s associated with negative stimuli, such as short social media clips (TikTok, Shorts, Instagram), or directly linked to pleasure (sex, food, interpersonal relationships)—but the reality is that it manifests in human history as something more raw.

Modernity is often criticized for its frenetic flow of information, and I agree that this is negative and exposes the average human—and even the cognitive elite of the species—to sensitive and unstable conditions. However, it’s important to be realistic about dopaminergic pathways, starting with what dopamine essentially is;

Dopamine can be summarized as useful information assimilated by the brain and predictive error; these two things are the results of an invariance, which is the process of predictive stability. Dopamine is the process that brings the body back to a stable and balanced state;

Sex (trigger of instability) -> stability process (dopamine) -> stabilization (other hormones)

TikTok (high-frequency stimulation that impacts the body) -> information assimilation process (dopamine) -> video saturation 

To make the relationship clear, sex imposes states of tension on the body (blood pressure, muscle tension, high levels of hormones related to libido in the brain and body)—all of which are extreme states of disorder in the body. Dopamine arises during the act precisely as a means of bringing the body back to stability (passive homeostasis).

Why, then, does it generate pleasure? This is relatively obvious when one realizes that no living being does anything for N = 0; dopamine is the evolutionary mechanism the body devised to guide the individual toward stability in a state of instability. Otherwise, we would likely just accept all instability until we completely collapsed—which is obviously not very evolutionary. It guides the process and sustains it.

Through these pathways, we examine cases of ADHD involving giftedness. A critical point I’ll make is that many of the associated clinical diagnoses serve only to reduce a single process into two distinct categories. ADHD is applied precisely to differentiate between two states that, in practice, are related: an individual who processes information more quickly is naturally able to extract information more rapidly from a video, a text, or sensory input.
This leads to a fundamental process: when dopaminergic pathways encounter a brief predictive error, they naturally manifest at too low a level; ADHD is merely a way to explain or classify this process.

People in the general population take longer to process information, so the dopaminergic state is maintained for a longer period. But to clarify this right away: if this were an absolute truth with no further context, gifted individuals would be frantic and the average person would not—but reality is different;

Why? The answer lies in the information: the average person takes longer to process information, just as logic is exhaustive. This leads us directly to why feeds are personalized—they introduce “preferences” that the person can process quickly. This shortens the dopamine window that the person craves so much, which leads them to watch faster-paced videos, This creates an obvious cause-and-effect relationship: a passive, low-density assimilation of information that sustains a cycle of predictive error and stability occurring at a rate of cycles per second. Naturally, no body can sustain this without consequences, leading to what many may have already observed as neurological changes in the brains of those who use TikTok or short-form feeds heavily.
